Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Android apps published on Google Play by Aleksandar Yasenov ("I", "me"), an independent developer based in Plovdiv, Bulgaria. If an individual app collects data beyond what is described here, its Google Play listing and in-app notice will say so.

Information I collect

My apps are designed to work without collecting personal information. I do not ask for your name, address or other identifying details, and I do not sell personal data to anyone.

Files and data you process inside an app (for example, files you choose to erase) stay on your device and are not uploaded to me or to any third party.

Permissions

Apps request only the Android permissions needed for their features (for example, storage access to erase files you select). Permissions are used solely on your device for that purpose.

Purchases and subscriptions

Paid features are handled by Google Play Billing. Payment details are processed by Google and are never shared with me. Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

Google Play may collect crash reports and usage statistics under Google's own policies. If an app uses any other third-party service (such as advertising or analytics), it will be listed in that app's description and data-safety section.

Children

My apps are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union, you have rights under the GDPR, including access to and deletion of any personal data about you. Because my apps do not collect personal data, there is usually nothing stored about you; if you email me, I will use your message only to reply and will delete it on request.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: aleksandar.yasenov.dev@pm.me
